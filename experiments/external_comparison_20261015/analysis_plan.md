# Analysis specification before final aggregates

Written on 2026-10-15 while the fixed confirmatory_v1 queue was running (88/384 jobs completed). No final or partial confirmatory method aggregates were inspected to choose this specification. The frozen planning/evaluation protocol remains unchanged. This document supplies the inferential details omitted from its shorter statistics entry; it is not represented as having preceded data collection.

The map is the independent inferential unit. Each risk planner has five seeds on each of 24 maps, with eight maps in each of three generator families. Primary reliability is joint_cfr_0.6, which assigns zero to absent or nominally infeasible outputs. Raw perturbed-path CFR and internal constraint satisfaction are separate diagnostics.

There are four primary tests: RiskAwareDE minus CC-VPSTO adaptation and RiskAwareDE minus SAA-AVaR adaptation, each for joint CFR and length. Reliability differences are between map means over all five seeds. Length differences are between map means over only the seeds for which both methods returned nominally feasible paths. Report paired seed counts and available map counts. A map with no eligible paired seed is excluded from that conditional length estimand and explicitly listed; no zero imputation. A* is descriptive only.

Use two-sided exact sign-randomization of the Wilcoxon signed-rank statistic, dropping zero differences and assigning average ranks to equal absolute differences. Compute the full sign distribution by dynamic programming with ranks multiplied by two, so ties are retained. This is a conditional random-sign test requiring symmetry of map differences, not proof that randomized algorithm seeds randomize the methods themselves. Holm-adjust the four primary p-values together. If a contrast is undefined, retain its place in the family with p=1 and clearly mark the estimand unavailable.

Report mean paired reliability change in percentage points and conditional mean paired length difference in coordinate units. Also report percentage length change as 100*(mean paired RiskAwareDE length / mean paired comparator length - 1), without giving it an additional hypothesis test. Construct percentile 95% confidence intervals using 20,000 map bootstrap resamples, independently within each of the three scene families and retaining their original available-map weights. Use fixed analysis RNG seed 202610150704. These intervals describe between-map sampling variation, not just 20,000-world Monte Carlo precision. No optional stopping, selective contrast deletion, or switching primary CFR definitions.

Descriptive outputs include joint/raw CFR, nominal feasibility, method-specific internal feasibility, joint nominal-and-CFR>=0.825 pass fraction, nominal-feasible length, solve time median/mean/p95, observed maximum process-tree RSS, family summaries and complete row-level data. Do not compare internal feasibility rates as equivalent chance guarantees: risk functionals and thresholds differ. Planning times are measured under the specified 820-evaluation versus six-second settings, and are not an equal-time superiority claim.

Audit completeness, protocol/source/map hashes, packed collision-bit counts, job failures and source-code immutability before releasing aggregates. Tightening the saved CC curve evaluation tolerance is a sensitivity analysis only; it does not replace the frozen primary scores or trigger replanning.
